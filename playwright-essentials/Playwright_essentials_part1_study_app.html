    Predict the outcome

    await page.waitForTimeout(5000) in every test → ?

    Slow, flaky suite; prefer auto-wait + assertions.

    expect(locator).toBeVisible() fails → ?

    Playwright waited up to timeout; check locator/trace.

    Spot-the-Bug

    🐞 page.click('.btn:nth-child(3)') only

    Brittle CSS; prefer getByRole/name.

    🐞 No webServer; tests hit random ports

    Configure webServer + baseURL in playwright.config.
