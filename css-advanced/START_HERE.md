## Study Order

1. **Part 1 — Motion**: Transitions vs. animations, keyframes, timing curves, `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`, and GPU compositing performance.
2. **Part 2 — Transforms & Layering**: 2D/3D transforms, `transform-origin`, stacking contexts (`z-index`), `isolation: isolate`, and `clip-path` shapes.
3. **Part 3 — Variables, Tokens & Preprocessors**: CSS Custom Properties (`--token`), Primitive/Semantic/Component token hierarchy, theme engine, and Sass/PostCSS overview.
